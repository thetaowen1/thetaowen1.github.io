Privacy

Optional visit statistics

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand referral sources, pages viewed, page interactions, approximate location, and browser or device information. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits. We do not enable advertising personalization or Google Signals in our website tag, and we do not intentionally send names, email addresses, or phone numbers to Analytics.

Analytics starts only after you choose “Allow analytics.” You may decline and continue using the website. To change your choice, return to the website and select “Privacy settings.” Declining later stops future analytics on this browser; it does not erase data already processed.
